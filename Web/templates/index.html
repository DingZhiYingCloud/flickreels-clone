He rushed to find her, and a hospital exam revealed years of old injuries. A DNA test confirmed with 99.9999% certainty that Gemma was the Whitmore family's long-lost daughter. As her abusers face justice, Gemma returns to her family's Manhattan penthouse and begins healing in the love of her parents.
